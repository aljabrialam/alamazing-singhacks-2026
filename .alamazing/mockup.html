What this version does differently. The argument is drawn rather than described. Four blocks in one hue at four strengths, collapsing into a single bar — they were always the same thing, only the labels differed. That figure explains the product to someone who has never seen a portfolio, in about two seconds.

The band chart earns its place because it shows a negative. Five green pins, all comfortably inside range. A judge sees compliance passing at a glance, then reads the 42% above it. Saying "no breach" is a claim; showing five pins inside five bands is evidence.

The opening line gets the only inverted panel on the page. Navy, full bleed, 29px. It is the last thing before the evidence and the thing worth remembering.
