Where we would not rely on ourselves

Three things we are not sure about

These did not meet the bar for a brief. Leaving them out would be the same as pretending we had not noticed.

We do not know whether Nordvind Industrial is in profit.
What we would check. No cost basis came through with Mrs Voss-Brenner's transfer, so the position carries no gain or loss. It is 18% of her portfolio and one of the obvious places to fund the tax instalment from — which makes this gap matter more than most.
The 42% figure treats the note as full exposure to all three names.
What we would check. A worst-of basket is not the same as owning three positions outright. The direction is not in doubt; the precise number depends on how the desk models the payoff. We would rather show the working than round it.
Private fund valuations are a quarter behind.
We have not adjusted for it. That is how the industry reports rather than an error, but it means the liquidity picture for the Fong sleeve could be better or worse than it looks today.

What changed. The earlier draft read like a trading console — dense rules, monospace, a dark grid. This is a private bank, so the reference is a printed advisory note: white sheets on a quiet ground, generous margins, and figures set in the same serif as the prose rather than in code type.
